<h1>OpenMATB Overview</h1>
<p>OpenMATB is a multi-task environment simulation software, first developed in 1992 by Comstock and Arnegard. This version was proposed by Cegarra et al. (2020).</p>

<p>Because it requires participants to control several tasks simultaneously, this software enables a detailed understanding of the mental processes involved in managing complex and dynamic situations.</p>
